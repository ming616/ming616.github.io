## Collaboration

The team actively seeks collaboration with universities, research institutes, medical institutions and industry enterprises, with focus on the following domain-specialized intelligent-service research and applications:

### Key Application Domains

1. **Intelligent Industrial Design & Engineering Drawing** — intelligent conversion between 3D industrial models and 2D engineering drawings, design-information understanding, engineering-rule constraints and result-consistency verification
2. **Smart Healthcare & Respiratory Critical Care** — using public data such as MIMIC-IV and eICU for multi-source clinical analysis of respiratory critical care, disease-specific intelligent analysis, medical-knowledge fusion and trustworthy, explainable intelligent services
3. **Smart Elder Care & Anomaly Behavior Detection** — daily-care monitoring, safety-risk identification and anomaly detection for the elderly, with multimodal sensing, anomaly recognition and risk analysis
4. **Intelligent Education & Teaching Services** — multi-agent-supported personalized learning, teaching-data analysis, learning-process management and intelligent teaching services
5. **Cross-Border E-Commerce Intelligent Services** — multi-agent intelligent services for market-information analysis, product-information organization, customer service and business operations

### Collaboration Interfaces

1. Joint research and grant applications in domain-specialized intelligent services, Agentic AI, knowledge representation, causal intelligence and reliability enhancement
2. Joint R&D of AI technology, agent systems and domain-specialized intelligent services for real industry needs
3. Industry data resources, representative application scenarios, intelligent-service evaluation benchmarks and reliability-assessment systems
4. Enterprise requirements analysis, software system development, industry application validation and results transformation
5. Co-supervised graduate training, undergraduate research practice, AI professional training and international academic exchange

Driven by real application problems, grounded in the fusion of domain expertise and intelligent technology, and focused on service reliability and practical value, the team looks forward to collaborating with partners on research, innovation and application.
