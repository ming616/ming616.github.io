## Team & Graduate Training

Based at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Zhongyuan University of Technology, and led by Prof. Xiaoming Liu, the team consists mainly of master's and undergraduate students, pursuing AI research, technology development, talent training and industry application.

### Composition

- **Supervisor**: Prof. Xiaoming Liu (Associate Professor, Ph.D., Master's supervisor)
- **Members**: enrolled master's and undergraduate students across NLP, multimodal, causal intelligence, video anomaly detection and intelligent software engineering
- **Compute base**: multiple servers with NVIDIA A800 and RTX 3090 GPUs, plus a localized large-model deployment and experiment environment supporting LLM inference, model quantization, multi-model orchestration and agent-system development

### Scale & Outcomes

- **30+** master's students supervised or co-supervised (including enrolled)
- Graduates mostly work in AI and internet enterprises in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou and Shenzhen; some join state-owned enterprises, central enterprises, universities and research institutes
- Alumni have worked at Baidu, Huawei, JD.com, 58.com, the Institute of Information Engineering of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and others

### Training Honors

- Supervised master's and undergraduate theses have received the **Henan Province Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis**, the **ZUT Outstanding Master's Thesis** and **Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis** awards
- Multiple undergraduates have gone on to leading universities such as Xidian University and Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications for graduate study

### Approach

Real-problem-driven research training: students of different years participate in academic research, algorithm experiments, software system development and industry practice, jointly strengthening research innovation, engineering practice and cross-disciplinary problem-solving.
