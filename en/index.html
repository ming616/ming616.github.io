Xiaoming Liu — Associate Professor, Ph.D., Master’s Supervisor

School of Artificial Intelligence, Zhongyuan University of Technology

Research on reliable, domain-specialized intelligent services driven by real industry needs — under constraints of data, compute, domain expertise and business rules, we leverage large models, agentic collaboration, knowledge representation, graph learning and causal intelligence to build reliable intelligent service systems for complex professional tasks.

Research Themes (see Research)

  • Domain-specialized intelligent services & multi-agent collaboration (Agentic AI) — task decomposition, collaborative planning, dynamic resource scheduling, model–tool collaboration
  • Reliability assessment & enhancement of intelligent services — failure discovery and capability enhancement across trustworthiness, faithfulness, interpretability, explainability, consistency and generalization
  • Knowledge representation, graph learning & causal intelligence — causal invariant learning, causal consistency, knowledge-constrained causal reasoning
  • Intelligent service software systems & engineering — runnable, testable and evaluable multi-model, multi-agent, multi-tool systems

Key Application Domains

  1. Intelligent industrial design & engineering drawing
  2. Smart healthcare & respiratory critical care
  3. Smart elder care & anomaly behavior detection
  4. Intelligent education & teaching services
  5. Cross-border e-commerce intelligent services
