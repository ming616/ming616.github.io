Xiaoming Liu

Associate Professor · Ph.D. · Master’s Supervisor · School of Artificial Intelligence, Zhongyuan University of Technology

Ph.D. in Computer Software and Theory from Beijing Institute of Technology (2013). Research focuses on natural language processing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, big data, knowledge representation and intelligent software engineering.

From February 2017 to February 2018, funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC), conducted visiting-research work at the University of East Anglia (UEA), UK. Has also conducted academic exchanges at multiple universities in the UK, Australia and Portugal.

Research Projects

In recent years, hosted or participated in multiple projects from the NSFC, the National Social Science Fund, the Henan Provincial Department of Education and other provincial/municipal sources, together with Ministry of Education industry-education collaboration projects and enterprise-commissioned R&D (see Projects).

Publications

40+ papers in NLP, knowledge representation, information extraction and machine learning, published in Neurocomputing, the Chinese Journal of Computers, Acta Electronica Sinica, the Journal of Computer Science and Exploration, the Journal of Chinese Information Processing and other venues (see Publications).

Peer Review & Service

Reviewer for Neural Networks, Neurocomputing, IJCAI, AAAI, and the journals Computer Science, Journal of Computer Science and Exploration, Computer Engineering and Applications, as well as the CCL and CHIP conferences.

Serves as a member of the Language Intelligence Committee (CAAI), the Youth Working Committee and the Medical Health & Bioinformatics Committee of the Chinese Information Processing Society; member of CCF, CAAI and the Chinese Information Processing Society.

Teaching

Long-term teaching of undergraduate and graduate courses (see Teaching).
