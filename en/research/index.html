Research

The team focuses on reliable, domain-specialized intelligent services driven by real industry needs: under constraints of data, compute, domain expertise and business rules, we combine large models, agentic collaboration, knowledge representation, graph learning and causal intelligence to build reliable intelligent service systems for complex professional tasks.

1. Domain-Specialized Intelligent Services & Multi-Agent Collaboration (Agentic AI)

Intelligent service systems based on large language models, multimodal models and multi-agent collaboration, focusing on:

  • Intelligent task decomposition and collaborative planning
  • Dynamic resource scheduling and model–tool collaboration
  • Complex task execution and business-process orchestration
  • Construction methods for intelligent services in real business scenarios

2. Reliability Assessment & Enhancement of Intelligent Services

Addressing erroneous dependence, reasoning bias, result inconsistency, insufficient explanation and reduced environmental adaptability in complex professional tasks, we study reliability assessment, failure discovery, behavior verification and capability enhancement across:

  • Trustworthiness
  • Faithfulness
  • Interpretability
  • Explainability
  • Consistency
  • Generalization

With particular attention to healthcare, industrial design and safety care, where critical failures are strictly constrained.

3. Knowledge Representation, Graph Learning & Causal Intelligence

Effective use of domain knowledge, graph structures and causal information in domain-specialized intelligent services, exploring:

  • Causal invariant learning
  • Causal consistency
  • Knowledge-constrained causal reasoning

to reduce erroneous dependence, improve reasoning consistency, and strengthen interpretability and cross-environment generalization.

4. Intelligent Service Software Systems & Engineering

Intelligent software systems for multi-model, multi-agent, multi-tool collaboration, exploring:

  • Model service management and model access
  • Intelligent task organization and execution
  • Task context management and collaborative scheduling
  • Reliable operation and continuous improvement

Grounded in real business problems and public data resources, we develop runnable, testable, evaluable and continuously improving prototype systems for method validation and industry application (see Collaboration).
